Your goal is to click on as many of these black dots as possible while also counting backward by 3 (series 1), then 7 (series 2), then 17 (series 3) from the numbers provided by the study coordinator. Please make sure to maintain counting as you click on the dots. Each series will last for 2 minutes. You will have a 90 second break between each series.

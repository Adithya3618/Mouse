Mouse Accuracy Study · Instructions

Instructions

You will complete 7 tasks total, consisting of a motor baseline followed by 3 series of cognitive and dual tasks, with 90-second recovery periods between series.

  1. Dot clicking on a screen — 2 minutes
  2. 90-second recovery
  3. Count backward by 3 — 2 minutes
  4. Dot clicking + count backward by 3 — 2 minutes
  5. 90-second recovery
  6. Count backward by 7 — 2 minutes
  7. Dot clicking + count backward by 7 — 2 minutes
  8. 90-second recovery
  9. Count backward by 17 — 2 minutes
  10. Dot clicking + count backward by 17 — 2 minutes

Total protocol time: approximately 19 minutes.

Follow the instructions shown on the screen throughout the experiment.
